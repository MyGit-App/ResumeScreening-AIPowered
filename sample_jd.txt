Position: Senior Data Scientist

Role overview:
Design, build, and deploy machine-learning solutions for business and product
problems. Work with engineering and product teams to turn data into reliable,
measurable outcomes.

Required qualifications:
- Five or more years of relevant data science or machine-learning experience.
- Strong Python and SQL skills.
- Hands-on experience with machine-learning frameworks such as scikit-learn,
  TensorFlow, or PyTorch.
- Experience with cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.
- Experience deploying and monitoring models in production.
- Knowledge of statistical analysis, experiment design, and communicating
  results to non-technical stakeholders.

Preferred qualifications:
- Experience with Spark or another distributed data-processing framework.
- Experience with Docker, Kubernetes, or MLOps tools.
- Familiarity with NLP, large language models, vector search, or RAG.

Responsibilities:
- Develop reproducible data and machine-learning pipelines.
- Evaluate model performance and communicate limitations.
- Collaborate with cross-functional partners on responsible deployment.
